FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What makes Exalt Growth different from other SaaS SEO agencies?
We combine traditional SEO with Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) for visibility across Google, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and AI agents. Unlike agencies that focus on traffic vanity metrics, we tie every initiative to demo/trial conversions and ARR impact.
How quickly will we see results?
Initial wins land within 60–90 days (LLM inclusions, snippet captures, traffic increases). Sustainable growth is typically evident around month 4–6, and full category dominance takes 9–12 months.
Do you work with early-stage startups or only later-stage companies?
We specialize in funded SaaS companies from Seed through Series B. If you've raised capital and are ready to build a sustainable organic growth engine, we're likely a good fit.
How do you measure success?
We track leading indicators (entity coverage, LLM citations, snippet captures) and lagging indicators (organic traffic, conversions, ARR attribution) with complete funnel dashboards.
Do you guarantee specific rankings or traffic numbers?
No ethical SEO agency can guarantee specific rankings. Instead, we guarantee our process: strategic discovery, entity-first execution, continuous optimization, and transparent reporting.
How hands-on is the founder involvement?
Very. Jack Boutchard leads strategy and execution on every engagement. You work directly with the founder, not junior account managers.
What's required from our team?
Minimal lift. Your PMM/content lead provides context and approvals (1–2 hours/week). Your eng/design team supports implementation (light lift, ~1 sprint/quarter).
Can you work with our existing content team?
Absolutely. We can operate as your complete SEO function, collaborate with in-house content teams, or train your team on our methodology.
